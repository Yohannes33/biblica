Amharic verse check

Biblica is a free app for memorising Bible verses. Its Amharic New Testament (1962) comes from a digital copy where, in some places, several verses were joined and only one verse's words survived. We want to put each surviving text back under its correct verse number, but only where a native reader agrees.

  1. Read the Amharic text, then the English of the verse we think it is.
  2. Press Yes if it is that verse. If it is another verse, press Different verse and pick it. If it is none of them, or mixes several verses, press None. Use Not sure freely: unconfirmed verses simply stay hidden in the app.
  3. Your answers are kept in this browser as you go. When you are done, or want to stop, send us the answers from the bottom of the page.
