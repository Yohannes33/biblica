# Biblica

## Register
product

## Users & Purpose
People memorizing Bible verses on their phone in short sessions (a bus ride, a coffee break, before bed), from teens in youth groups to adults keeping a daily habit. Biblica is an installable PWA: open it, play a 2–5 minute round (fill-in-the-blank, flashcards, word scramble, trivia), watch the streak and XP move, close it. The verse text is the content. Everything else is the game around it.

## Brand Personality
Playful, rewarding, warm. A game you want to come back to tomorrow. It feels like a native iOS app, not a website. The verse itself is treated with care: set in a calm serif, never decorated. Reward moments (a finished stage, a streak, a combo) get real celebration.

Scene: someone on a phone, one-handed, in daylight on a bus or under a dim lamp at night. That means both themes are needed, and every tap target has to work for a thumb.

## Anti-references
- Generic blue/indigo SaaS dashboards and Tailwind default gradients.
- Cards nested inside cards; a chip or badge on every element.
- Hero-metric stat grids; tiny 10px uppercase labels.
- Church-website beige/parchment clichés.
- Desktop-style centered modals on a phone.

## Design Principles
1. The verse is the hero: big serif text, generous line height, no chrome competing with it.
2. Every tap feels tactile: buttons press down, correct and wrong answers say so in colour, motion and sound.
3. Native-feeling shell: safe-area aware bars, bottom sheets, segmented controls, 44px+ targets.
4. Reward is gold and earned: gold is used only for XP, streaks and achievements, never as decoration.
5. One visual vocabulary: the same button, tile and sheet everywhere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast in both themes, visible focus rings, keyboard reachable, accessible names on icon buttons and dialogs, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, pinch-zoom allowed.
